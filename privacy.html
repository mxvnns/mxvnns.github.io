Privacy policy

MXF MASTERMIND · effective 31 October 2026

Who this covers

MXF MASTERMIND is a personal dashboard that runs on one computer and is used by one person: its owner. It only ever connects to its owner's own accounts. Nobody else signs in to it, and it offers no service to anyone else. Contact: mxvnns@gmail.com.

What each connection reads

Every connection is read only. Nothing is ever posted, uploaded, commented, liked, followed, messaged, sent, deleted or changed on any account.

YouTube (Google)

Permissions: youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly. Reads the owner's channel name and subscriber count, daily views and estimated revenue, and the newest videos' titles, dates and view counts. Public comments on the owner's own videos are read with an API key; commenters' names are not kept.

Gmail (Google)

Permission: gmail.readonly, nothing else. Searches the owner's mailbox for brand deals, payments and platform notices. For the emails it keeps, it stores the sender, the subject and the date, and Gmail's short preview line only for brand deals, payments and platform notices. It never stores an email's body or attachments. On the owner's click it can summarise one email; that summary is not kept.

TikTok

Permissions: user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list. Reads the owner's display name, follower, like and video counts, and each video's title, date, link and its view, like, comment and share counts.

Instagram

Permissions: instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_insights. Reads the owner's professional account profile, follower count, account views and reach, and each post's and reel's date, caption, link and its views, likes, comments, shares and saves.

Facebook

Permissions: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, read_insights. Reads the owner's own Facebook Page: its name, followers, views, and each video's and reel's title, date, link and views. It never reads a personal profile, friends or messages.

Roblox

Reads public player counts for the owner's games and games the owner follows, and, with the owner's own read-only key, the owner's games' analytics from Roblox (players, Robux earnings).

Where it is kept

  • Everything it reads is kept in files on the owner's computer. There is no MXF MASTERMIND server and no database anywhere else.
  • Sign-in tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on that computer and are never sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.
  • The dashboard's own data, without sign-in tokens, is backed up daily to the owner's private GitHub repository.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to profile anyone.

What leaves the computer

  • The platforms themselves: requests to read the data above.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): the owner's questions to the dashboard's assistant, with the figures needed to answer them; an email the dashboard's own rules cannot sort (the sender's name and domain, the subject and the preview line); and one email the owner asks to summarise. Email addresses, links, phone numbers and codes are removed first.
  • A text-to-speech service: the assistant's spoken replies, as text.
  • Telegram: alerts the owner switched on, sent only to the owner's own phone.
  • GitHub: the owner's private backup.

Google user data

MXF MASTERMIND's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to show the owner their own information in this dashboard. It is not used for advertising, it is not read by people other than the owner, and it is not used or transferred to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

How to disconnect and delete

  • In the dashboard: Settings, Connections, then Disconnect on any platform. This cancels the access at the platform and deletes that platform's sign-in token from the computer. Disconnecting Gmail also deletes every email record it kept.
  • At Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions, then remove MXF MASTERMIND OS or MXF Mail.
  • At TikTok: in the app, Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then the apps and services that have access, and remove MXF MASTERMIND.
  • At Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, then remove MXF MASTERMIND.
  • At Facebook: Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations (or Apps and websites), then remove MXF MASTERMIND.
  • Anything else: the stored readings live only on the owner's computer and in the owner's private backup, and are deleted by deleting those files. To ask about any of it, write to mxvnns@gmail.com.

Children

MXF MASTERMIND is not directed at children and is not used by anyone but its owner.

Changes

This page changes when a connection changes. The date at the top says when it last did.
