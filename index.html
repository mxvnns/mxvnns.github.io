MXF MASTERMIND

A personal dashboard. One computer, one user: its owner.

What it is

MXF MASTERMIND is a private dashboard that runs on its owner's own computer. It brings the owner's creator stats into one place so they can be read at a glance. It is not offered to anyone else: there is no sign-up, no account to create, and nobody uses it except its owner.

What it does

  • Shows the owner's YouTube channels: views, subscribers, estimated revenue and the newest videos' views.
  • Shows the owner's Roblox games: player counts, and Robux earnings from Roblox's own analytics.
  • Shows the owner's TikTok, Instagram and Facebook Page: followers and how many views each video gets.
  • Finds brand deals, payouts and platform notices in the owner's Gmail, so they are never missed.
  • Adds the views and the revenue from every platform into one total.

Read only, always

Every connection only reads. MXF MASTERMIND never posts, uploads, comments, likes, follows, messages, sends, replies, deletes or changes anything on any account. It asks each platform for the narrowest read-only permission that platform offers.

Names on sign-in screens

When the owner connects an account, the platform's sign-in screen shows one of these names: MXF MASTERMIND OS (YouTube), MXF Mail (Gmail), and MXF MASTERMIND (TikTok, Instagram and Facebook). They are all this dashboard.

Privacy

What each connection reads, where it is kept and how to disconnect is in the privacy policy. The rules for using it are in the terms.
